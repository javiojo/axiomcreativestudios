Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Axiom Creative Studios (“we”) is a bilingual agency serving Orlando and Central Florida. This page explains, in plain language, what happens to information when you use axiomcreativestudios.com.

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us through the contact form on this site: your name, email address, optional phone number, the service you are interested in, and your message.

How the contact form works

When you submit the form, your message is sent through FormSubmit, a third-party form service, which delivers it to Axiom’s email inbox. FormSubmit processes the message in order to deliver it. We use what you send only to reply to you and to discuss the services you asked about.

Spam protection

To keep out spam, the form uses a hidden field that people never see, a short math question, a minimum-time check, and a limit on the number of links in a message. These checks run in your browser and do not track you. Messages that fail the checks are discarded and are not delivered to us.

Fonts, cookies and tracking

This site hosts its own fonts and images, so it does not load Google Fonts or other third-party font services. We do not run advertising trackers, and we do not use analytics or advertising cookies on this site. Your browser may contact FormSubmit only when you send the contact form.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with the service providers needed to deliver your message, such as FormSubmit and our email provider, and where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your message for as long as needed to respond and to keep a record of our conversation with you. You can ask us to delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have from you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask us to stop contacting you. Use the contact details below.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Use the contact form, email address and phone number on our home page, or call 321-446-2526.
